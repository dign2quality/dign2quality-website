About the Book

Why This Book Exists

Manufacturing complexity scales faster than attention. A single overlooked process change, supplier shortcut, or unverified measurement can cascade into program delays, warranty charges, and reputational damage. Yet the reverse is also true: when prevention, supplier integration, and learning become the operating system, reliability compounds into margin, market preference, and resilience.

Built to Last is written for every person who touches manufacturing—from the technician who stops the line to the CEO who decides where to invest scarce capital. It offers a pragmatic, experience-driven blueprint for moving quality off the margins and into strategy.
