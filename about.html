The Story Behind the Book

After 20 years in manufacturing quality management, I got tired of watching the same movie play out over and over again.

A supplier ships defective parts. Production uses them anyway because they're behind schedule. Six months later, customer returns start flooding in. Engineering scrambles to identify root cause. Quality gets blamed for "not catching it." The CFO cuts the quality budget to "improve margins."

Rinse and repeat.

I've seen this pattern destroy companies. Good companies. Companies with talented people who wanted to do the right thing but didn't have the frameworks, tools, or—most critically—the organizational buy-in to prevent problems instead of just reacting to them.

Here's what frustrated me most: the answers already exist. DFMEA, PFMEA, CAPA, COPQ, supplier quality management—these aren't new concepts. They're proven methodologies that work when implemented correctly. But they're often trapped in consultant-speak, academic papers, or $500,000 enterprise software implementations that only Fortune 500 companies can afford.

I wrote Built to Last because I wanted to create the resource I wish I'd had on day one—practical guidance that doesn't require a PhD to understand or a Fortune 500 budget to implement.

Why I Wrote This Book

I got tired of seeing quality positioned as the enemy. The "quality cop" who slows down production. The "bad guy" who rejects parts. The overhead cost that needs to be minimized.

That's backwards.

Quality should be your competitive advantage, not your scapegoat.

When you build quality into your processes from the start—when you prevent failures instead of just detecting them—something remarkable happens:

  • Production becomes faster (because you're not stopping to fix problems)
  • Margins improve (because scrap, rework, and warranty costs plummet)
  • Customers become loyal (because they get reliable products)
  • Your team becomes proud (because they're building things that last)

This book is my attempt to share what I've learned over 20+ years—the frameworks that actually work, the tools you can use Monday morning, and the mindset shift that turns quality from overhead into advantage.

I want to change the conversation. Stop fighting fires. Start preventing them.
